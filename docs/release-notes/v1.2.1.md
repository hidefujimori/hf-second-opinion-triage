(SHA-256 `2da0d35e4442f84e2b9f3875a0e493e1f9737f109c550194eb4466628f38987e`).

## Packaging fixes for Splunkbase
- `app.conf` `[package] check_for_updates = 1`: Splunk can tell you when a newer version is on Splunkbase.
  Splunkbase requires it.
- `app.manifest` `platformRequirements`: `Enterprise` is `*` instead of `>=9.3`. The Splunk Packaging Toolkit
  rejected the old value ("includes no supported version"). The app still needs Splunk 10.x and ES 8.7 or later.

No functional change.

## Upgrade from 1.2.0
Apps > Manage Apps > *Install app from file* > `second_opinion_triage-1.2.1.spl`, tick *Upgrade app*
(Splunk Cloud: Apps > Upload app). Nothing to configure; settings and results are kept.
