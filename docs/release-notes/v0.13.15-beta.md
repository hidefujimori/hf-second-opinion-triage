# HF Decision Model Triage and Second Opinion App v0.13.15-beta (public beta)

[日本語は下にあります](#日本語)

Changes since **v0.13.13-beta**. Package: `second_opinion_triage-0.13.15.spl`
(SHA-256 `560944c476895ded4c1465d4b03fc0841007c6d84c50ba55a266ecc6d4dec58c`).
AppInspect (cloud tags, precert): 0 failures, 0 errors.

## Highlights: urgency
Urgency often came out one level high. Two decision rules and clearer urgency criteria fix most of it.

### Decision rules (0.13.14)
- **Undetermined is at most Medium** (`[jev] undetermined_urgency_cap = medium`). A finding not known to be real
  needs checking today; "likely compromise" (High) does not fit it.
- **Critical needs a clear answer** (`[jev] critical_min_probability = 0.60`). When Jev's probability for Critical is
  below this (a near coin toss between High and Critical), the urgency is High.
- The reason is recorded in `jev_policy`. Empty / `0` turns a rule off.

### Urgency criteria (questions `q-2026-11-04-r4`)
- **High** now includes covering tracks on hosts (Security event logs cleared, user accounts deleted on several hosts).
- **Medium** now includes read-only discovery of resources, even by a suspicious account.
- **Critical** is impact in progress (data theft or destruction, mass resource abuse) or administrator access granted
  to an account an attacker controls (e.g. a new IAM user added to an administrator group by a role session from an
  outside network, or an access key created for that user). "Deleting access keys or logs" is no longer a Critical
  example.
- The disposition questions are unchanged. If you use your own questions (support mode), they stay in use; compare
  them with the new defaults before switching.

## Upgrade from 0.13.x
1. Apps > Manage Apps > *Install app from file* > `second_opinion_triage-0.13.15.spl`, tick *Upgrade app*.
   On Splunk Cloud: Apps > Upload app. Restart Splunk if asked.
2. Nothing to configure. To keep the previous urgency behaviour, set `undetermined_urgency_cap =` (empty) and
   `critical_min_probability = 0` in `local/jev_triage.conf`.

Settings and results are kept. Urgency of new triage results may be lower than before for Undetermined findings and
borderline Critical ones.

## Requirements
- Splunk Enterprise 10.x with ES 8.7, or Splunk Cloud with ES 8.7.
- A key for the decision model (TypeSafe Jev by default) and, for explanations, an LLM key — your own keys,
  billed by those providers.

## Tested on
- Splunk Enterprise 10.4.2 + ES 8.7.1 (BOTS v3 data, 33 labelled findings), Jev 1.13: disposition 32/33, no true
  positive missed (unchanged), urgency 30/33, within one level 33/33.
- Splunk Cloud 10.4 + ES 8.7.1: installation (0.13.13).

## Known limitations
- Urgency criteria were tuned on a small set of labelled findings; check them on your own labelled data.
- Threat intelligence matches cover source IP addresses only (not domains, URLs or hashes yet).
- The built-in thresholds for `clef` and `gpt-6-luna` were set before these changes; re-check before switching models.
- Not yet listed on Splunkbase (planned for 1.0).

## 主な変更: 緊急度（Urgency）
緊急度が1段階高めに出ることが多くありました。判定のルール2つと、緊急度の基準の書き直しで大半を直しました。

### 判定のルール（0.13.14）
- **判別不可は最大 Medium**（`[jev] undetermined_urgency_cap = medium`）。本物か分からない Finding は「今日中に確認」で、
  「侵害の可能性が高い」（High）には当たりません。
- **Critical ははっきりしたときだけ**（`[jev] critical_min_probability = 0.60`）。Jev の Critical の確率がこれ未満
  （High と Critical がほぼ五分五分）のときは High にします。
- 理由は `jev_policy` に記録します。空 / `0` でそのルールを使いません。

### 緊急度の基準（質問 `q-2026-11-04-r4`）
- **High** に、ホスト上で痕跡を消す行為（セキュリティのイベントログの消去、複数ホストでのユーザーアカウントの削除）を含めました。
- **Medium** に、資源を読み取るだけの探索（怪しいアカウントによるものでも）を含めました。
- **Critical** は、被害が進行中（データの持ち出し・破壊、資源の大量の悪用）、または攻撃者が支配するアカウントへの管理者権限の付与
  （例: 外部ネットワークのロールのセッションが新しい IAM ユーザーを管理者グループに追加、そのユーザーのアクセスキー作成）としました。
  「アクセスキーやログの削除」は Critical の例から外しました。
- Disposition の質問は変わりません。独自の質問（サポートモード）を使っている場合はそのまま使われます。切り替える前に新しい既定と
  比べてください。
