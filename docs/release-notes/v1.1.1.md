# HF Decision Model Triage and Second Opinion App v1.1.1

[日本語は下にあります](#日本語)

Changes since **v1.1.0**. Package: `second_opinion_triage-1.1.1.spl`
(SHA-256 `7884dfd602ccbfd97b06b9483a8a165f7046d2ed71aeda3146d8ee8521b1292e`).
AppInspect (precert, all checks and cloud tags): 0 failures, 0 errors.

## Fix
- Long text without spaces (certificate hashes, Windows paths, URLs, defanged addresses) ran out of its column in
  the detail pane and over the next column, most visibly in translated or full ES AI texts. It now wraps inside the
  column (ES AI, Jev and decision guide columns). Normal text wraps as before.

## Upgrade from 1.1.0
Apps > Manage Apps > *Install app from file* > `second_opinion_triage-1.1.1.spl`, tick *Upgrade app*
(Splunk Cloud: Apps > Upload app). Nothing to configure; settings and results are kept.

## Tested on
- Splunk Cloud 10.4 + ES 8.7.1: the detail pane of a finding with long ES AI texts (translated).

## 修正

  しました（ES AI・Jev・判断ガイドの列）。普通の文章の折り返しは変わりません。

（Splunk Cloud では Apps > Upload app）。設定は不要で、設定と結果はそのまま残ります。
